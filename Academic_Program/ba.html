Welcome to Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

The Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) program is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in humanities, social sciences, literature, history, political science, economics, and cultural studies. This 3-year undergraduate course is divided into six semesters (1st to 6th Semester) and helps students develop critical thinking, communication skills, creativity, and a deep understanding of society and human values. The program also prepares students for higher education, competitive examinations, and various professional career opportunities.
