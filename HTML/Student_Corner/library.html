Library

The library is called a heavenly place, a temple of knowledge. In any educational organization, the library serves as an ornament. The library building of the college has been committed for a long time to meet the diverse needs of scholars, readers and students while meeting the criteria set by the University Grants Commission. The library has been a vast collection centre of 47164 books, providing books on various subjects of the college such as Arts, Commerce, Computer Science, Management and the latest Faculty of Science for the benefit of students.

Apart from books, the library acquires 20 magazines and 03 newspapers as well as subject annuals, special issues for the preparation of competitive examinations.

For the convenience of general readers, the facility of Internet Center and Network Resource Center for the dissemination of technical subjects along with a spacious well-equipped reading room is being provided free of cost in the library.

The SOUL software has also been used in the library, achieving remarkable success in the direction of self-government. Work is in progress to digitize the library.
