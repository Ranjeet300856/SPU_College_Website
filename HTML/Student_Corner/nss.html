NSS

The main objective of the National Service Scheme is to develop their personality through their education as well as social service and become patriotic and capable citizens

By associating with NSS, students can become aware of many important topics such as national integration, AIDS, literacy, environment protection, women development and upliftment and wasteland development, development programs run by the nation/state etc. and thus become a responsive citizen in the society.

In NSS, students are required to work for 240 hours in their field of interest in two years as part of regular activities. In the regular activities, students have to participate in a seven-day special camp on various topics, rehearsals, debates, improvisations, quizzes, awareness etc. Volunteers doing excellent quality work are awarded and honored at the national level every year.

In our college, the state government has given NSS to the college. One unit of the National Institute of Educational Empowerment (CBDT) is sanctioned for which 100 students are enrolled every year. No registration fee is payable for participating in NSS activities but every interested student/student is required to fill an application form. In this regard

National Service Scheme Programme Officer Dr. Magan Lal Ji can be contacted.
