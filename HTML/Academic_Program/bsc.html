Welcome to Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.)

The Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) program is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in scientific knowledge, research, analytical thinking, and practical learning. This 3-year undergraduate course is divided into six semesters (1st to 6th Semester) and covers various subjects such as physics, chemistry, mathematics, biology, and other science-related disciplines. The program helps students develop problem-solving abilities, laboratory skills, and scientific understanding, preparing them for higher education, research opportunities, and successful careers in the science and technology fields.
