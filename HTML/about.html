History of Institution

S.P.U. (P.G.)A warm welcome to the college. This educational institution holds a special place in the middle of the rural area of Godwad region. As a result of the generosity and generosity of the Jain community, this institution germinated. This institution was born in Ummedpur as a middle school with the inspiration and guidance of the late Yugveer, Punjab Kesari Acharya Shrimad Vijay Vallabhsurishwar Ji Maharaj and his disciple Ratna Shri Lalit Surishwar Ji Maharaj. Later, it was transferred to Falna station. As a result of the continuous efforts of education lovers, this inter college was established in 1951 and in 1958, due to the tireless efforts of Acharya Pravar Shri Samudra Surishwerji Maharaj Saheb and Munibhushan Marudhar Ratna Shri Vallabh Vijayji Maharaj Saheb, it was developed as a degree college. In 1986, the college received the postgraduate level. From the session 2003-04, he has completed B.B.A. under Computer Science and from the session 2008-09. The course began. For the all-round personality development of the students, various co-educational activities are conducted every year through various councils formed in all the faculties, as well as employment counselling cell, women cell, planning forum, raging prevention center etc. are also being operated.

The college is run by Shri Parshwanath Umaid Jain Shikshan Sangh, Falna. This institution has the blessings of Acharya Shri Indradinna Surishwerji Maharaj Saheb. He has been affiliated to Jai Narain Vyas University, Jodhpur since the session 2012-13. Admission to this institution is available for all classes. Over the last 69 years, the college has always strived to establish traditions and a high standard of education and cultural achievement.
