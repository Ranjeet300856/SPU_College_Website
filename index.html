Welcome to S. P. U. (P.G.) College, Falna


S.P.U. (P.G.) College , Falna is a Prime Higher Education Institution not only in the Jodhpur Division, but also in the State of Rajasthan . This College is a part of S.P.U. JAIN SHIKSHAN SANGH, FALNA, The Leading Educational Institution in Gaurwar Region.


The College is affiliated To J. N. V. University, Jodhpur . The College has around 65 Bigah land area and is situated on Falna-Bali Main Road in Gaurwar Region in Pali District of Rajasthan. Students from Bali, Mundara, Sadri, Desuri, Ghanerao, Bijapur, Sewari, Bhatoond, Lunawa, Nana, Moribeda, Pindwara, Sumerpur, Takhatgarh, Sanderao, Rani, Varkana, etc., come here for their studies.


College runs B.Sc.(Maths, Bio), B.A., B.Com., B.C.A., M.Sc. (Zoology).
