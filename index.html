Welcome to S. P. U. (P.G.) College, Falna


S.P.U. (P.G.) College , Falna is a Prime Higher Education Institution not only in the Jodhpur Division, but also in the State of Rajasthan . This College is a part of S.P.U. JAIN SHIKSHAN SANGH, FALNA, The Leading Educational Institution in Gaurwar Region.


The College is affiliated To J. N. V. University, Jodhpur . The College has around 65 Bigah land area and is situated on Falna-Bali Main Road in Gaurwar Region in Pali District of Rajasthan. Students from Bali, Mundara, Sadri, Desuri, Ghanerao, Bijapur, Sewari, Bhatoond, Lunawa, Nana, Moribeda, Pindwara, Sumerpur, Takhatgarh, Sanderao, Rani, Varkana, etc., come here for their studies.
See History of Institution


College runs B.Sc.(Maths, Bio), B.A., B.Com., B.C.A., M.Sc. (Zoology).

Student Notice

Consider your college as a sacred temple of Saraswati and behave cordially with due respect towards your teachers and classmates.
While remaining self-disciplined, take full advantage of the resources and facilities available in the college and keep them safe like your personal property.
While taking interest in studies, make the programmes organised for the development of your personality successful by attending them.
Maintain the dignity of the college, because your personal interest also lies in it.
Be regularly present in your classes for studies.
Do not use mobile phones in the classroom. Keep your mobile switched off.
Follow the rules made for the smooth functioning of the college with full devotion.
